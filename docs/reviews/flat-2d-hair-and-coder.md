# Flat 2D hair and coder review

Manifest 1.12.0 adds a reusable side part. The original sweep and wave choices
remain available; the straight-fringe crop is removed. Proposed spikes and curls
did not pass review and were removed as well. Every template exposes the three
retained styles.

Coder retains its beanie and code emblem identity while moving from purple hat,
purple hair, and crop fringe to a charcoal beanie, cyan emblem, cocoa hair, and
side-part default. This aims for a quieter technical association without
introducing a one-off hat or changing the coder role alias.

Use `pnpm review:assets` to generate a small contact sheet for the current
decision, normally at 64 and 256 pixels. Expand to more states or templates only
when a visible issue needs that comparison. Approved snapshots remain untouched
until the new direction is explicitly accepted.
