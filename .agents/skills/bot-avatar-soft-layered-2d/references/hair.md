# Hair

- Pick one main difference: part position, bang direction, or side length. Build
  it with broad connected masses rather than strand lines or repeated tiny tufts.
- Keep the distinction visible below the hat at 64 pixels. A new outline hidden
  entirely inside the crown does not create a useful instance choice.
- Treat front and back hair as a pair. Preserve the face opening and eye clearance;
  inspect side joins, exposed roots, and intersections with the brim.
- Check the new hair under a beanie and a brimmed hat. Inspect the bucket fit when
  its lower brim intersects the silhouette; add explicit fitting only where needed.
- Compare candidates in the same hair color and hat before judging palettes.
  Preserve existing options unless removal or replacement is requested.

Use `side-part.svg`, `sweep.svg`, and `wave.svg` under
`assets/parts/soft-layered-2d/hair/` as current construction references, together
with their catalog back-hair and fitting mappings. Removed crop, spikes, and curls
are not selectable references. Rejection of those implementations is not a ban on
all future short or curly designs; a new proposal needs its own visual review.
