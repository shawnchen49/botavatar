# Hats

- Start from the nearest existing crown and brim geometry. Change the silhouette
  needed for the requested hat; preserve the shared head scale and face opening.
- Establish crown and brim before seams or decoration. The basic hat type should
  remain readable with the emblem and detail lines hidden.
- Fit the lower edge over the hair: inspect gaps, exposed hair roots, and side
  collisions. Check both a compact and a wider hairstyle when the brim changes.
- Place a licensed monochrome emblem within the actual hat panel, with clear
  space from its seams and brim. Reuse fitted mounts; do not redraw the icon.
- Printed emblems stay on the surface. Tangible accessories may use separate
  layers and receiver-clipped shadows under ADR 0010; do not raise every detail.
- Review beside a neighboring hat with the same hair, background, and state.
  Keep new designs as optional templates unless replacing a default is requested.

Use current assets under `assets/parts/soft-layered-2d/hats/`. The cap and beanie
provide simple construction references; pilot parts demonstrate fixed accessories.
The superseded occupational redraws are not approved alternatives to this family.
